SageTV MiniClient Dev v0.5.60

Host/MCP-only update. No APK rebuild required.

Changes:
- Adds --hardware-only as a convenience alias for --decoders hardware.
- Hardware-only full matrix contains 21 cases.
- Player-matrix Comskip now sends short native SageTV RIGHT/LEFT commands and observes recovery with host-side video/audio counters.
- Removes the long Android BroadcastReceiver Comskip observation path from the full player matrix, avoiding ~60-second infrastructure failures while video is already healthy.
- Comskip landing position is diagnostic-only.

Recommended hardware-only command:
./dev.sh mcp-player-matrix --server 192.168.10.175 --text "meet the press" --hardware-only --watchdog-ms 180000
