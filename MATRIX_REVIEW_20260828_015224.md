## Strong trap evidence

In a long Media3-family Pull case, `pull_seek_reprepare_before` fired at roughly 10 s, followed by `pull_seek_reprepare_after`; first video frame did not arrive until roughly 34 s after that reprepare. The reset therefore discarded/duplicated useful progress instead of accelerating recovery.

Exo2 Pull telemetry now shows the large Comskip involves tens of MiB of synchronous SageTV READ traffic. At the 5 s milestone it had already accumulated ~6.1 s of READ wait; final operation telemetry was ~67.6 MiB / 258 READs / ~6.96 s READ wait. This makes Exo2 network block size a measurable next variable.
