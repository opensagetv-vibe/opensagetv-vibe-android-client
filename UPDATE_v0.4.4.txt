SageTV MiniClient Dev v0.4.3 -> v0.4.4

Purpose: repair Fire TV video output lifecycle after real hardware testing showed:
- Legacy Exo exits when starting video
- Media3 audio-only
- IJK audio-only

- No internal telemetry hooks.

  ./dev.sh validate
  ./dev.sh build
  ./dev.sh install
