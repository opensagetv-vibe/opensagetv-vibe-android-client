SageTV MiniClient Dev v0.5.26

Reverts the temporary v0.5.25 playback-bisect rollback and restores the v0.5.24 Android session-control source.
Adds ./dev.sh mcp-playback-test for explicit server/player configuration and native Meet the Press startup.

Default:
  server:    192.168.10.175
  player:    media3
  streaming: dynamic (Push/Dynamic)
  decoder:   hardware

Run:
  ./dev.sh mcp-playback-test --server 192.168.10.175 --player media3 --streaming dynamic --decoder hardware

Native video-start sequence:
  command search
  delay 50
  sendtext meet the press
  delay 50
  sendkey BACK
  delay 100
  sendkey FAST_FORWARD
  sendkey RIGHT
  sendkey PLAY_PAUSE
  sendkey PLAY_PAUSE

This test does not use Sagex/HTTP to start playback.
