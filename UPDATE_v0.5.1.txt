SageTV MiniClient Dev v0.5.1 update

Fixes IJK/GSY MPEG-2 playback on Amazon AFTMM / mantis Fire TV.

- Prevents the confirmed MTK MPEG-2 MediaCodec dequeueInputBuffer failure loop.
- IJK/GSY automatically use bundled FFmpeg software decode for MPEG-2 on AFTMM/mantis.
- H.264/AVC, HEVC, MPEG-4 and other devices keep normal hardware decoding.
- Adds a second codec-selector guard plus regression tests/validation.

Apply over the v0.5.0 project root, then run:
  ./test_valitdate_build_install_lanuch.sh

First runtime check:
  Select IJKPlayer, keep Decoding Method = Hardware, and replay the same MPEG-2 recording.
